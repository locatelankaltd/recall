# Original Recall artwork

Original vector interim; one Flora exploration completed and reviewed.

These vectors were constructed specifically for this Recall rebuild. They are not downloaded from Mobbin, an icon library, or generated through Flora. No claim of trademark clearance, legal uniqueness, or store approval is made.

Flora exploration: `../flora/recall-icon-exploration.png`, generated through `mcp__flora__flora_create_generations` using `t2i-flux-2-klein-4b`; run `run_m17c5qmyvtncstcdasv43qwvcs8gwgjy`. One batch, one 1024 × 1024 output, quoted 4 credits / $0.004 and charged $0.004. Actual credits charged were not separately returned. Project sharing is restricted. The image was viewed and is retained as concept evidence, but its unwanted texture/shadow and weak fold geometry make it unsuitable as the final app icon without further work. No additional generation was submitted.

Functional SVG masters are in ../icons. Flutter renders the matching original paths through CustomPainter, with no SVG package dependency. The app mark is a folded page with an open shoulder forming a lowercase r.

PNG files are deterministic local rasterizations of the controlled original SVG geometry. App-icon artwork has a solid opaque background; platform corner masking is applied by iOS/Android. The transparent Android adaptive foreground and monochrome master retain safe-zone padding.
