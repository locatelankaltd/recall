Leave on your terms.

Delete your cloud account in Recall

  1. Open Recall and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Open your account from the workspace or settings screen.
  3. Select Delete account and cloud data.
  4. Review the confirmation, enter your password to authenticate again, and confirm deletion.

Successful cloud account deletion removes the account and its server-managed projects, proposals, review links, responses, service limits and entitlement records. Recordings indexed to that account on the current device are also cleaned up. A connection or cleanup failure is shown in the app; an unsuccessful request is not reported as completed.

A one-way security marker remains to prevent late requests from recreating deleted account data. It contains no email address, source text or project content.

Device workspaces and exports

Private device projects are separate from your account. Use Delete local workspace in settings to remove that workspace. Delete exported JSON or recording copies from wherever you saved them. Recall cannot remove copies held by other people or files outside its storage.

Subscriptions

Account deletion does not cancel an app-store subscription or erase store purchase records. Cancel through your store's subscription settings.

If you cannot access your account

First try the password-reset action. You can also request deletion assistance at locatelankaltd@gmail.com from the account email address. Account ownership must be verified before a support-assisted deletion. Never send a password.

Service-provider security logs and backups may follow the provider's retention processes. Optional AI material already processed by a provider follows its applicable processing and retention settings.
