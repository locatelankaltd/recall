Your sources. Your choice.

This page describes the data flows implemented in Recall. Optional services are available only when configured in the app.

Private device workspaces

You can use Recall without an account. Device projects are not automatically uploaded. Mobile project text uses platform secure storage. Recordings are ordinary app-private files; they are not encrypted by that text-storage service. Browser storage has different protections. Exported workspace files contain your project and source text, and exported audio contains your recording.

Account workspaces

Firebase Authentication processes your account email and sign-in credentials. Firebase Firestore stores account projects, proposals, source text, review responses and service limits. Server functions validate account access. Device recording paths are excluded from cloud project records.

Client review links

Anyone holding a valid review link can open its selected changes and the source evidence included in that review. Share links only with intended reviewers. Links expire and can be revoked. A response records a link-holder action; it does not verify the person's identity. Minimal response receipts can remain available after a link closes, without reopening source evidence.

Optional AI

Recall asks before sending selected text or a whole recording for analysis or transcription. When enabled, server functions send the approved material to the configured OpenAI service. AI output is a draft and can be wrong. Review it before using it. Provider processing and retention are governed by the provider's applicable service settings and policies; Recall does not promise zero provider retention.

Subscriptions and reminders

When store subscriptions are configured, the app store and RevenueCat process purchase information and an account identifier. Recall uses a server-verified entitlement. Recall does not receive your card details. Optional deadline reminders are scheduled on your device and use generic notification text.

Deletion and controls

You can delete projects, revoke review links, export your workspace and delete your cloud account in the app. Local device data is managed separately. Account deletion does not cancel an app-store subscription. A one-way marker without your email or project content is retained to prevent late requests from recreating deleted data. See the account deletion instructions for details. Platform security logs or backups may follow the service provider's retention processes.
