Cloud sign-in

Use the verification email after creating an account, then return to Recall and refresh your account status. Use the password-reset action on the sign-in screen if needed. Your private device workspace is separate from your signed-in account workspace.

Recordings and exports

Recording permission is optional. You can still type or import source text. Workspace JSON exports do not include audio; export recordings separately before changing devices. Browser recording copies should be saved before leaving the session.

Review links

A link may be expired, revoked or refer to a proposal that is no longer current. Ask the project owner for a new link. A closed review cannot be used to change a newer version.

Billing

When subscriptions are available, manage cancellation and renewal through the app store used for the purchase. Deleting a Recall account does not cancel a subscription. Optional AI and subscription services may be unavailable until configured.
